Postitheka

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Postitheka lets you write a post once and publish it to the social accounts you connect. This page says what it keeps to do that, why, where, for how long, and how to have it deleted. It covers the website at postitheka.facorreia.com and the Postitheka iOS app.

Postitheka is run by Fernando Correia, who is responsible for your data (the data controller). Write to fernando@facorreia.com about anything on this page.

What we keep

WhatWhyHow long
Your email address, and Apple's id for you if you sign in with Apple To identify your Postitheka account. With Sign in with Apple, the address is the one Apple shares, which can be a private relay address if you chose to hide yours. Until you delete your account.
Your sign-in tokens To keep you signed in. We store only a one-way hash of each token, never the token itself. Until you delete your account.
Connected accounts To publish where you choose. For each account you connect (for example a Discord webhook, or a LinkedIn, Threads, Instagram, Facebook Page, X or TikTok account): the platform's id and display name for it, and the access token or webhook address the platform gives us. Tokens and webhook addresses are encrypted before they are stored and are never shown back, logged or sent anywhere except to that platform. Until you ask us to remove the account, or delete your Postitheka account.
Your posts To publish them and show you how each one went: the text, the photos and videos you attach, when to publish, the options you set per destination, and each destination's outcome (published, failed, the link to the published post). Until you delete your account. Files uploaded but never used in a post are deleted within about an hour.
Usage events To know whether the product works: that an account was created, that an account was connected (and to which platform), and that a post reached or failed to reach a platform. Each event carries a random account id and the platform name. It never carries your email, your post's text, your files or a platform's error message. As kept by our analytics provider (see below).
Server logs To run and secure the service: request paths, times, status codes and errors. Kept briefly, then overwritten.

We do not show ads, sell or rent your data, or track you across other sites and apps. Postitheka sets no advertising cookies. The website keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage; the iOS app keeps it in the iPhone's Keychain.

When we act on your connected accounts

Postitheka posts to a platform only when you publish or schedule a post and choose that account as a destination. It reads from a platform only what it needs to connect the account and publish (such as your account's name and id, or the Facebook Pages you can post to). When you connect an account, the platform shows you what it lets Postitheka do; you can withdraw that permission at any time in the platform's own settings.

For WhatsApp and Reddit, Postitheka posts nothing itself: the iOS app hands your post to that app on your phone, and you send it.

Who else handles your data

  • Hetzner (Germany) hosts the server and the database.
  • Apple, if you sign in with Apple: Apple confirms who you are to us. We receive Apple's id for you and the email address it shares, nothing else.
  • Cloudflare R2 (Western Europe) stores the photos and videos you upload.
  • PostHog (EU region) receives the usage events described above.
  • The platforms you publish to (Discord, LinkedIn, Meta for Threads, Instagram and Facebook, X, TikTok) receive the posts you send them, under their own privacy policies.

We share your data with no one else, unless the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. If you are in the EU, the UK or a similar jurisdiction you also have the rights to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority; in Portugal that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt). We rely on our contract with you (providing the service you asked for) for your account, connected accounts and posts, and on our legitimate interest in knowing whether the service works for the usage events and logs.

Deleting your data

To delete your Postitheka account and everything in it, write to fernando@facorreia.com from the email address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We delete your account, your connected accounts and their tokens, your posts and your uploaded files within 30 days, and confirm when it is done. Posts already published on a platform stay there until you delete them on that platform.

To stop Postitheka using one platform account only, remove Postitheka's access in that platform's settings, or write to us and we will delete that account's details.

Children

Postitheka is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly keep their data.

Changes

We will update this page when what we collect or why changes, and change the date at the top.
