# Create social media posts from the approved app videos

Act as a product launch marketer and social media copywriter. Read `marketing/prompts/00-video-production-rules.md`, `marketing/strategy.md`, `marketing/features.md`, `marketing/rules/video-playbook.md`, and the approved scripts and exports for the full showcase, UGC ad, feature videos, and teaser.

## Goal
Prepare a coordinated **social media publishing package**. Default to **one primary video per post**, with one audience problem and one CTA. Do not assume every video is finished: identify script-only, in production, approved, and export-ready states separately.

For each finished or planned video, write:
- An engaging platform-appropriate post title or first sentence.
- A LinkedIn caption (helpful, product-oriented, appropriately professional).
- An Instagram Reels / TikTok caption (shorter, conversational, strong hook).
- A YouTube Short title and description if a vertical export exists.
- One CTA linked to the approved destination only; if no actual URL is known, use `[APP URL]` placeholder.
- Up to five relevant hashtags where appropriate, without spam.
- Thumbnail headline, optional first-frame text, accessibility-friendly alt text or video description, and any disclosure required for an AI presenter or other promotional context.

Propose an approximately two-week release sequence with sensible spacing and distinct creative angles. Include a mapping of post to specific exported file; do not propose publishing an unrendered asset as if it exists. Avoid invented engagement forecasts or trending hashtags. Update `marketing/rules/content-calendar.md` and per-video `social-post.md` files. Provide a concise approval checklist, **but do not schedule or publish automatically**.
