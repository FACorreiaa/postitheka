# Produce Video 2 — AI presenter / UGC advertisement

Act as a UGC ad creative director, marketing copywriter, presenter director, and editor. Read and obey `marketing/prompts/00-video-production-rules.md`. Start with `marketing/strategy.md`, `marketing/features.md`, `marketing/rules/video-playbook.md`, `marketing/videos/production-plan.md`, and the available asset manifest. Reuse approved footage and branding from Video 1.

## Goal
Create a **30–45 second 9:16 AI presenter advertisement** for the actual app. It must feel natural and credible, not like a generic slideshow or fictional testimonial. Choose an appropriate presenter style and voice for the documented audience, but do not imply the presenter is a genuine user or employee.

## Story arc
- **0–5s — Hook:** One immediate user problem or motivating question.
- **5–12s — Introduction:** App name, what it does, one concrete value proposition.
- **12–30s — Demonstration:** Presenter intercut with real captured UI footage demonstrating two or three *verified* features.
- **30–40s — Payoff:** How the features combine to solve the problem; no unverified numeric claims.
- **Final 5s — CTA:** App name, approved CTA, clear brand shot.

Use frequent visual changes, clean captions, natural speech, and legible screen demonstrations. Use presenter-only or picture-in-picture compositions when useful; switch to full-screen real UI when precise controls matter. Keep music unobtrusive and use appropriately licensed audio.

## Execute
1. Inspect the planned Video 2 storyboard, ensure it fits the runtime, and reconcile it with available footage.
2. Create/update `marketing/videos/ugc-presenter-ad/brief.md`, `script.md`, `storyboard.md`, `shot-list.md`, `generation-plan.md`, and `social-post.md`.
3. Identify any missing recording by screen, flow, platform, and exact click/tap sequence; capture it only if a safe demo environment is available.
4. Inspect actual Higgsfield presenter/video generation options and tool availability. Propose the scene-by-scene generation settings and estimated credits, or mark prices unknown.
5. **Stop for explicit approval before spending credits.** After approval, generate presenter clips and supporting visuals, combine with authentic footage, add narration/captions/music, and export a final 9:16 MP4 if the available tools allow it. Optionally produce a 16:9 adaptation only if requested or useful and feasible.
6. Perform QA, log exactly which final files exist and where, and prepare one standalone post caption with hook and CTA. Do not publish.

If a required tool is unavailable, deliver the ready-to-use provider prompts and a precise editing timeline rather than claiming the video was rendered.
