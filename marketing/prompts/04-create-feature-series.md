# Plan and produce a series of feature showcase videos

Act as a series producer. Read `marketing/prompts/00-video-production-rules.md`, `marketing/strategy.md`, `marketing/features.md`, `marketing/rules/video-playbook.md`, and `marketing/videos/production-plan.md` if present.

## Goal
Create a coordinated **three-video series**, one 20–30s video per major feature. Do not combine all three into one long advertisement. Make each clip independently understandable and publishable. Reuse the style and approved assets from the full app showcase.

1. Rank currently available features by viewer value, distinctiveness, visual demonstrability, and platform coverage. Select three *different* capabilities; record your rationale.
2. For each selected feature, follow the process in `marketing/prompts/03-create-feature-showcase.md`: identify exact real interactions, write a script and timestamped storyboard, plan footage, create a reusable shot list, prepare an individual caption and CTA.
3. Produce a single consolidated capture plan to minimize redundant app navigation, plus a shared assets/reuse plan.
4. Create three separate folders under `marketing/videos/feature-<short-slug>/`, and update `marketing/videos/production-plan.md` with status and dependencies.
5. If footage or safe capture tooling is missing, state the blockers and give precise capture instructions.
6. Summarize generation scenes, actual tool availability, and estimated Higgsfield credits. **Request explicit approval for each batch of paid generation**. Do not spend credits simply because the series plan was approved.
7. After production is authorized, generate/edit/QC **one feature video at a time** and log each verified output. Stop on unexpected cost, availability, or quality problems.
8. Write three distinct social posts with different hooks and explain a reasonable publish order. Do not publish anything without explicit permission.
