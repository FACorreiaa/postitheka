# Produce Video 6 — 10–15 second promotional teaser

Act as a short-form creative director and motion designer. Read `marketing/prompts/00-video-production-rules.md`, marketing strategy/features/brand, the video playbook, and the approved footage from the showcase and feature videos.

## Goal
Create a **10–15s vertical 9:16 branded promotional teaser**. This is an attention-grabbing first impression, not a complete tutorial. The audience should understand what the app is for within the opening seconds.

## Structure
- **0–2s:** Punchy, accurate headline that expresses a user problem or product benefit.
- **2–9s:** Fast but legible montage of two or three *actual* app UI moments; optionally use Higgsfield for supporting device or background transitions.
- **9–12s:** Clear single benefit or differentiation that is documented and supportable.
- **12–15s:** App identity, logo, and CTA.

Use no invented feature demonstrations, fake customer quotes, or misleading visuals. Keep on-screen text readable on a phone. Use approved fonts/colors/branding. Avoid re-generating footage if clips from earlier productions will suffice.

## Execute
Prepare `marketing/videos/promotional-teaser/brief.md`, `script.md`, `storyboard.md`, `shot-list.md`, `generation-plan.md`, and `social-post.md`. Propose the exact asset sources, timeline, music rights, and any Higgsfield generation and cost estimates. **Pause for approval before paid generation.** After approval, render and verify the 9:16 MP4 when supported; log final assets, provide the social post, and do not publish.
