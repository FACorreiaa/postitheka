# Build or refresh the reusable video playbook

Act as a marketing operations lead and creative director. Read `marketing/prompts/00-video-production-rules.md`, `marketing/strategy.md`, `marketing/features.md`, existing `marketing/rules/video-playbook.md` (if any), any approved Video 1 storyboard and assets, and the brand documentation.

Create or update `marketing/rules/video-playbook.md` so that future production agents need minimal additional guidance. Include:
- Product description and audience (referenced rather than copied excessively).
- Visual identity: colors/fonts/logo usage based on existing evidence; mark unspecified values as to-be-confirmed.
- Voice and tone, narration and presenter guidelines.
- Repeatable structures for full app showcase (45–60s), AI presenter ad (30–45s), feature video (20–30s), teaser (10–15s).
- Portrait and landscape composition, subtitles, readability, shot duration, transitions, sound, and visual effects.
- App footage capture checklist for web and iOS.
- Where shared footage, logos, music licenses, assets, prompts, exports, and source manifests live.
- Video naming conventions, production statuses, sign-off stages, cost/approval gates, and QA standards.
- How new features should update the marketing documentation and trigger an optional video refresh.

Preserve approved existing creative decisions, highlight inconsistencies, and keep rules tool-agnostic except for documented provider-specific steps. This task is **documentation-only**: do not generate paid assets, modify product code, or publish.
