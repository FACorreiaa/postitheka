# Repurpose a finished marketing video into platform variants

**Source video:** [INSERT VERIFIED PATH OR SELECT FROM MARKETING ASSET MANIFEST]

Act as an experienced video editor specializing in SaaS product marketing. Read `marketing/prompts/00-video-production-rules.md`, the original video's storyboard, approved brand direction, and its source footage/export metadata.

## Goal
Create platform-appropriate versions from **existing approved footage** rather than generating another full video from scratch:
- **16:9, 1920×1080** for YouTube / website where appropriate.
- **9:16, 1080×1920** for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok.
- Optional concise 10–15s cutdown for social discovery.

Do not simply center-crop interfaces. Reframe shots, move/punch in on important UI, shorten or rewrite on-screen text, and reposition subtitles/CTA so everything remains readable. Preserve the source clip's facts, brand identity, and approved user flows. Where source video quality or assets do not support a format, report the issue rather than upscaling recklessly or inventing new screens.

## Execute
1. Check the source video actually exists and is approved.
2. Produce a timestamped adaptation/edit plan for each target format.
3. Prefer local editing of existing assets; propose a paid Higgsfield re-generation only if necessary, and **stop for approval before charges**.
4. Render available variants, check audio and subtitles, verify app UI visibility and CTA safe areas, and record exact output paths in the manifest.
5. Report which versions are genuinely rendered versus only planned. Do not publish.
